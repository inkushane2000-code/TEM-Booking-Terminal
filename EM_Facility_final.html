Instruments

Two core instruments are available in the EM Facility. The facility operator handles instrument operation; users should arrive with samples and documentation ready for the booked session.

Specimen preparation

Glow Discharging Units

The Tedpella and Quorum glow discharging units treat grids and substrates by modifying surface wettability, helping aqueous specimens spread evenly and adhere reliably before imaging.

  • Glow discharge treatment for TEM grids and substrates
  • Improves sample spreading and adhesion
  • Use the approved facility protocol for treatment settings
  • Completed signed User Form required
Grid preparation

Leica Dryer

The Leica Dryer is used to dry grids after sample application, allowing liquid to evaporate in a controlled way while the specimen settles evenly on the grid.

  • Controlled drying of prepared microscopy grids
  • Supports consistent specimen deposition
  • Confirm drying settings with the facility operator
Transmission electron microscopy

Talos L120C

The Talos L120C is a 120 kV transmission electron microscope for routine imaging, screening, and analytical applications.

  • Suitable for routine imaging and sample screening
  • Supports analytical microscopy workflows
  • Confirm specimen type and required grid/sample format before booking
Knife preparation

Leica Glass Maker

The Leica Glass Maker heats and shapes glass strips to produce clean, consistent glass knives. These knives are then used for ultrathin sectioning in the ultramicrotome.

  • Creates glass knives for ultrathin sectioning
  • Supports repeatable knife preparation
  • Use approved glass strips and facility procedures
Precision specimen preparation

Leica UC7 Ultramicrotome

The Leica UC7 uses a prepared glass or diamond knife to cut resin-embedded specimens into ultrathin sections for TEM analysis.

  • Precision sectioning for resin-embedded specimens
  • Produces ultrathin sections for TEM analysis
  • Resin block and suitable knife must be ready
Scanning electron microscopy

Quanta 200 SEM

The Quanta 200 SEM provides high-resolution surface imaging and elemental analysis for biological and material specimens.

  • Scanning electron microscopy for surface morphology
  • Supports imaging across multiple operating modes
  • Confirm specimen mounting and coating requirements

Booking Policy

Booking opens Friday at 12:00 PM and closes Sunday at 11:00 AM for the upcoming Monday–Friday. Slots are first-come, first-served. One operator manages the facility, so one instrument booking makes the corresponding time slot unavailable on every other instrument.

Cancellation & No-show

Cancel as early as possible so another user can use the slot. Repeated late cancellation or no-show may be reviewed by the facility. The facility operator may release or reassign slots when operational circumstances require it.

Booking Requirements

Provide student/user name, PI name, phone, email, specimen type, instrument, date, slot, and the signed Internal or External User Form. There is no training requirement because instrument operation is performed by the facility operator.
